# Privacy policy

**Last updated: 2026-11-01**

London Trainer is a chess opening trainer for Android, made by TBS Enterprises. It runs entirely on your phone.

## What data does London Trainer collect?

**None.** London Trainer does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data, analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or any other information from your device. The app contains no analytics or advertising code, and it does not ask for internet access, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

## What stays on your phone?

Your training progress, such as which positions you have seen and which ones to repeat, is saved privately on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it. If you use Android's device backup, your phone may include this progress in that backup. That backup is handled by your Google account, not by us.

## Permissions

London Trainer does not request any permissions.

## Children

London Trainer is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data, it collects none from children.
